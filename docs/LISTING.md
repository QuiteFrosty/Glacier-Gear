## Full description (body)

```
## Glacier Gear

Diamond-tier ice gear that actually does something diamond gear can't.

**Frostbite** — every hit with the Glacier Sword or Glacier Axe stacks Frostbite on the target.
Stack it high enough (IV) and they freeze solid where they stand.

**Glacial Pulse** — wear the full Glacier armor set and you're immune to freeze/hypothermia
damage. Drop below 30% health and it automatically fires a frost nova, hitting every hostile mob
within 6 blocks with Frostbite and knockback. 30 second cooldown — it's there when you need it.

**Chilling Touch** — the Glacier Pickaxe and Shovel have a chance to freeze nearby water into ice
as you mine. Small, but it's free terraforming.

**Ice Extractor / Packed Ice Extractor** — mine ice or packed ice with these and get a bonus Ice
Shard on top of the drop, for anyone stocking up on crafting material.

### Getting started
Craft Ice Shards and Ice Cubes with an Ice Extractor, then build the full Glacier armor set and
tool kit from there. Full crafting tree is on the Gallery/Recipes tab.

### Compatibility
Currently NeoForge 1.21.4. Forge and Fabric ports are in progress — see the Versions tab for
what's published.
```
